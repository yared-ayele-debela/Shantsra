Important Safety Guidelines

Inspect All Content

Travelers must physically inspect all packages before checking them into their luggage. Never accept closed, gift-wrapped, or mystery items.

Verify Government ID

Use the chat interface to request a verification of the passenger's boarding pass / passport and the sender's local government ID.

Check Airport Restrictions

Ensure that the goods do not violate customs regulations of Dubai (UAE) or Addis Ababa (Ethiopia). Strictly observe weight limitations.

Coordinate Safe Handover

For high-value items, perform handovers in public places (e.g. airport lobbies, registered hotels) and use digital payment escrow systems.
